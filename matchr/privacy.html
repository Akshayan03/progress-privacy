Matchr Privacy Policy

Matchr · Last updated 12 November 2026

Matchr is a dating app for adults, made by Akshayan Jeyakumar ("we"). To introduce you to people, Matchr has to store your profile on a server and show parts of it to other members. This page explains exactly what is stored, who can see it, and how to delete it. Matchr has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no data brokers, and we never sell your data.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), or, if you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier and email address Apple shares (which may be a private relay address), and the first name Apple shares if you allow it. We also note when you agreed to the Terms of Use.
  • Profile: first name, age (calculated from the birthday you choose; the birthday itself isn't stored), gender, who you'd like to meet, what you're looking for, city, photos, an optional voice intro, optional prompt answers, and the answers you choose to give about values and lifestyle (for example relationship goals, children, drinking and smoking).
  • Matching: your preferences and dealbreakers, whether you're shown in discovery, which mode you use, and, in The One, your questionnaire answers, introductions, round answers and whether you felt you clicked.
  • Activity: who you like and pass on, your matches, your messages, and the people you block or report.

Some of these answers can reveal sensitive information, such as your sexual orientation (from who you'd like to meet) or your beliefs. You choose whether to give them, and they are used only to show you compatible people.

Location

If you allow it, Matchr reads your location once, while the app is open, to fill in your city. Apple's geocoding service turns the location into a city name. Only the city is saved; your exact location is never stored or shown. You can type your city instead and never allow location access.

Camera, photos and microphone

The camera and photo picker are used only when you add profile photos, and the microphone only when you record a voice intro. Only the photos and recording you choose to save are uploaded.

Who can see what

  • Other members who fit your preferences (and whose preferences you fit) can see your first name, age, city, photos, prompts, voice intro and the answers you chose to give. Matches can also see the messages you send them.
  • Never shown to other members: your email, your dealbreakers, your exact location, who you passed on, blocked or reported.
  • Photos and voice intros are stored at unlisted web addresses so the app can load them quickly. Anyone who has the exact address could open the file, so share only what you're comfortable with.
  • Us: we can access stored data to run the service, to review reports of abuse, and when required by law.

Service providers

Matchr's data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage), hosted in the United States (AWS us-east-2). Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple. These providers process data only to provide their service to us. Matchr contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Safety and moderation

When you report someone, we store the report, the reason, any details you add and the conversation it relates to, so we can review it. Matchr automatically rejects messages and profile text that contain slurs, explicit sexual solicitation or threats, and hides a profile from discovery while it's under review. We may remove content and ban accounts that break the Terms of Use.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile › Delete account. This permanently deletes your login, profile, photos, voice intro, answers, likes, matches, messages and introductions from our servers straight away. Reports you made, and reports about you, are kept without your account attached so we can still act on them. Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule.

You can change or remove most information directly in the app, and you can pause your profile to stop appearing in discovery. To request a copy of your data or ask a question, email us.

Age

Matchr is only for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly allow anyone younger to use it, and we delete accounts we learn belong to minors. Please report any profile that looks underage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and we'll tell you in the app if the change is significant.
