Matchr Support

Help, safety and contact

Need help, want to report a safety concern, or have feedback? Email akshayanjeyakumar@gmail.com. We reply within two business days, and review safety reports within 24 hours.

Report or block someone

On someone's profile, tap the flag button; in a conversation, open the menu at the top and choose Report or Block. Reports are private: the person isn't told who reported them. Blocking is permanent; you stop seeing each other and any conversation ends. If you're in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

Dating safely

  • Keep conversations in Matchr until you're comfortable, and never send money or financial details.
  • Meet for the first time in a public place and tell a friend where you'll be.
  • Arrange your own transport and stay in control of your drinks.
  • Trust your instincts. You can leave or unmatch at any time.

Common questions

Why don't I see anyone? Matchr only shows people whose preferences you fit and who fit yours. By default it looks in your city and its metro area. Tap Show people everywhere, or open Profile › Preferences & dealbreakers to widen your age range or turn off dealbreakers.

What are demo profiles? A few clearly labelled demo profiles let you try likes, matches and The One while there are few members near you. They aren't real people.

How do I pause my profile? Profile › turn off Show me in discovery. Existing conversations stay open.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?. If the email doesn't arrive, email us from the address on your account.

How do I delete my account? Profile › Delete account…. This permanently deletes your profile, photos, voice intro, matches, messages and login.
