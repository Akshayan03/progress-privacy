PRogress Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026

PRogress ("the app") is a workout tracking app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short because the app's data practices are simple: your data exists to make the app work for you, and for nothing else.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we store your email address (used only for authentication) and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the account identifier those services provide.
  • Profile information. The display name, username, and bio you choose. Your username and display name are visible to other users of the app (for example in search and the workout feed).
  • Fitness and workout data. The workouts you log — exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, duration, personal records, and any notes you add.
  • Social activity. Who you follow, and workouts you like.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data.
  • No health data from Apple Health or other sources — only what you type into the app.
  • No contacts, messages, or browsing history.

How your information is used

  • To provide the app's features: logging workouts, tracking personal records, and the social feed.
  • Workouts you mark as public are visible to users who follow you. You can make any workout private, and you can change the default in Settings.
  • We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), acting as our service provider. Google may process basic technical information (such as IP addresses) as part of operating these services securely. See Firebase's privacy documentation for details. Access to your data is protected by security rules: only you can modify your own records.

Sign in with Apple and Google

If you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, authentication is handled by Apple or Google respectively. The app receives only your account identifier and email address (with Apple, you may choose to hide your email). The app never sees your password for these services.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, workouts, personal records, likes, and follows. If you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes its access to your Apple ID. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Children

PRogress is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
