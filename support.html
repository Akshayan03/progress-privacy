PRogress Support

PRogress is a workout tracker for people who lift: log sets fast, follow a Coach plan toward a lift goal, and see your records and trends.

Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Settings → Delete account. Everything stored in your PRogress account is deleted immediately.
  • How do I connect or disconnect Apple Health? Settings → Connected apps → Apple Health. To change what PRogress may read or write, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → PRogress.
  • How do I switch between lb and kg? Settings → Units. Your logged weights are kept exactly; only the display changes.
  • How do I report or block someone? On their workout in Friends, open the menu and choose Report or Block.
  • Who can see my workouts? Workouts set to "Friends can see" are visible to people who follow you; private workouts are only visible to you. Change the default in Settings → Workouts.
  • Is Coach medical advice? No. Coach gives general strength-training guidance from your own logged sessions.
