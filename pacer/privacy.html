Pacer Privacy Policy

Pacer: Run Coach & Plans · Last updated 9 November 2026

Pacer is a running app made by Akshayan Jeyakumar. In short: Pacer does not collect any data. Everything the app stores stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code.

What Pacer stores on your iPhone

  • Your runs: date, time, distance, duration, pace, splits, the GPS route, and heart rate and energy when they come from Apple Health or an imported file. Notes and the runs you hide or remove are stored too.
  • Your training plan: your goal, race date, the days you can run and the planned workouts.
  • Settings: units, voice and haptic preferences, and similar choices.
  • Widget and Live Activity data: a small summary of this week's plan, shared with Pacer's own widget through an app group on the device, and the current run's time, distance and pace while you run.

This data is kept in the app's storage on your device. Pacer does not use iCloud and does not send it anywhere. Your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, if you use them) may include it, as with any app.

Location

Pacer uses your location only while you are recording a run, to measure distance, pace and splits and to draw your route on the map. With your permission it keeps doing this while the screen is locked during a run. Pacer never tracks your location outside a run. The route is saved with the run on your device. Maps are drawn by Apple Maps.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional, and Pacer works without it.

  • Reading: if you allow it, Pacer reads your running workouts (including Apple Watch runs and runs other apps save to Health) with distance, duration, energy, heart rate and route, to build your history, records and training plan.
  • Writing: if you allow it, Pacer saves the runs you record in the app to Apple Health as running workouts with distance and route. If you remove a run that Pacer saved to Health, Pacer also deletes that workout from Health. Pacer never deletes workouts written by other apps.

Health data stays on your device. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties and never stored in iCloud by Pacer. You can change Pacer's Health access at any time in the Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Pacer.

Imported files

If you import a FIT, GPX or TCX activity file (for example one exported from a watch), Pacer reads it on your device and saves the run in the app. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

Third parties

Pacer contains no third-party SDKs: no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking. Pacer has no direct connection to Garmin or any other watch maker's service. Garmin is a trademark of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries; Pacer is not affiliated with Garmin.

Deleting your data

You can remove individual runs in the app. Deleting Pacer from your iPhone deletes all of its data. Runs that Pacer saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there (or remove them in Pacer first).

Children

Pacer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes
