Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Pacer has no sign-in. Your runs and plan stay on your iPhone.

How do I get a training plan?

Choose a goal during setup, or later on the Plan tab: a race (5K, 10K, half marathon or marathon), general fitness or getting faster. Pick the days you can run and Pacer builds a week-by-week plan. If you have recent runs in Pacer or Apple Health, the plan starts from your current running.

Why does the goal check say my target is a stretch?

Pacer estimates your current fitness from your recent runs. If your target time is well beyond that, it shows a realistic range so you can choose to keep the goal or adjust it.

GPS distance looks wrong or a run is missing its route

Run outdoors with a clear view of the sky and set Location access for Pacer to "While Using the App" (or "Always" so tracking continues reliably with the screen locked) in the Settings app › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Pacer. If a run is clearly wrong, swipe it in Progress to hide or remove it so it doesn't affect your records or plan.

My Apple Watch runs aren't showing up

In Pacer, turn on Settings › Apple Health › Import runs from Apple Health. Then check the Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Pacer and allow Workouts, Distance, Heart Rate and Workout Routes.

Can I use Pacer with a Garmin watch?

Yes, indirectly. Pacer has no direct connection to Garmin. In the Garmin Connect app, turn on sharing to Apple Health; Garmin runs then reach Pacer through Apple Health. You can also export a FIT, GPX or TCX file and open it in Pacer (Progress › Import). Garmin may not share the GPS route with Apple Health. Garmin and Garmin Connect are trademarks of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries; Pacer is not affiliated with Garmin.

The voice coach is silent

Check that Voice coach is on in Settings › During a run, that your ringer volume is up, and that no other app is holding the audio.

How do I delete my data?

Remove individual runs from Progress. Deleting the app deletes all Pacer data on your iPhone. Removing a run that Pacer saved to Apple Health also removes it from Health; after deleting the app, Pacer's runs stay in Health until you delete them there.

Health note

Training plans and paces are general guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting or changing an exercise program, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
